# 🚗 AutoSalesTrack

AutoSalesTrack is a modern and comprehensive software solution for managing car dealership operations. It combines real-time vehicle inventory tracking, streamlined sales processing, and integrated customer relationship management (CRM) to enhance efficiency and improve customer satisfaction.

---

## 📌 Features

### 🔐 User Management
- Secure registration and login for admins, staff, and customers
- Role-based access control
- Profile management

### 🚘 Inventory Management
- Vehicle catalog with details (make, model, year, color, VIN)
- Real-time stock updates
- Low-stock alerts

### 💼 Sales Management
- Complete sales workflow: lead → quotation → order → payment
- Quotation and invoice generation
- Sales tracking and order history

### 🤝 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Customer profiles and purchase history
- Lead tracking and follow-ups
- Feedback and reviews

### 💳 Finance and Payment
- Secure online payments
- Financing options and monthly payment calculators
- Automatic invoice generation

### 📈 Reports & Analytics
- Sales performance and revenue reports
- Inventory turnover and stock levels
- Customer behavior and demographic analysis

### 📣 Marketing & Promotions
- Promotional campaigns and discount management
- Targeted email marketing
- Social media integration

### 🧪 Test Drive Management
- Schedule and manage test drives
- Track test drive history

### 🔧 Service & Maintenance
- Track service history for each vehicle
- Customer service appointment scheduling
- Automated maintenance reminders

### 🔐 Security & Access Control
- Role-based module access
- Secure storage of customer and transaction data

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

- Develop an intuitive and responsive UI
- Provide real-time data insights
- Support customer engagement and retention
- Improve operational efficiency through automation
- Ensure data privacy and security
